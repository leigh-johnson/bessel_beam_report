\section{Results}

A suitable candidate meeting all design requirements was not found using 1 in. diameter axicon lenses with base angles $\alpha_1 = \alpha_2 = \SI{5}{\degree}$ and $\alpha_3=0.\SI{5}{\degree}$. The measured Bessel zone for each configuration is summarized in \autoref{tab:results_summary} and \autoref{fig:measured_window_envelopes_lobe_diam_colored}, along with the modeled geometric and QDHT ranges. Four candidates were evaluated, with a description of each in \autoref{tab:configs}. In three out of four candidates, the Bessel zone

The measured Bessel zone was imaged using a monochrome CMOS camera mounted on a motorized 3-axis CNC gantry, which allowed us to sample the beam profile along \SI{2}{\meter} of propagation with repeatable sub-millimeter positioning. See \autoref{sec:experimental_setup} for details on the experimental setup and optics design candidates. The core diameter and FWHM were extracted from $J_0^2$ fits within the measured Bessel zone (see \autoref{fig:placement_cmp_report_jul29}).

Candidate \#1 was the baseline configuration, with a measured Bessel zone length of $\Delta Z = 0.38 \pm 0.005$ m, within $\approx$ 1 cm of the length predicted by the QDHT model. The measured Bessel zone range $z_R \in [1.29, 1.68]$ m sits $\approx$ 10 cm from the predicted range. The measured core diameter and FWHM were within 2\% of the ideal $J_0^2$ value, with a mean core diameter of \SI{126.4}{\micro\meter} and FWHM of \SI{59.2}{\micro\meter}.

\section{Appendix A: Optics Design Candidates and Experimental Setup} \label{sec:experimental_setup}
Axicon lenses in \autoref{tab:configs} were sourced from Thorlabs (AX255-A, AX2505-A), manufactured from UV Fused Silica substrate ($n \approx 1.4565$ at 650nm) with AR coating for 350-700 nm range.

An adjustable focus laser diode (Thorlabs CPS650F) was coupled to a single-mode fiber using a fiber optic coupler (Schafter+Kirchhoff 60FC-L-4-M25-24) to produce a Gaussian input beam with $1/e^2$ diameter of $D_{\text{in}} = \SI{4.59}{\milli\meter}$ at axicon~1. The laser diode wavelength was nominally given as $\lambda = \SI{650}{\nano\meter}$, with a measured $\SI{1.2}{\milli\watt}$ power output after the fiber coupling.

Axicon lenses were mounted in XY translation mounts for alignment, and the axicon separations $L_{12}$ and $L_{23}$ were measured using a stainless steel ruler.

The Bessel zone was imaged using a monochrome CMOS camera (FLIR BFS-PGE-31S4M-C) with 2048x1536 resolution with a \SI{3.45}{\micro\meter} pixel size. The camera was mounted on a translation stage to image the Bessel zone at different $z$ positions.
